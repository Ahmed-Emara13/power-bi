Before and after

Building a report used to take a specialist and a cycle. Now it takes a sentence.

A good report needs someone who knows Odoo’s data, Power BI’s formula language (DAX) and report design. Every change request went back to the same person.

How it works

From a request to a live report in five steps.

  1. Describe it

    Pick the workspace and dataset, then write what you want in plain language.

  2. The AI reads the dataset

    Google Gemini studies its tables, columns and links, and drafts a report built for your request.

  3. Review the blueprint

    See every formula and chart before anything is published. Untick what you don’t want.

  4. Build & Push

    One click installs the formulas and creates the report in Power BI, bound to your data.

  5. Open and reuse

    Open it in Power BI or inside Odoo, and save the design to reuse for the next client.

What the AI prepares

A complete report, not a list of suggestions.

A title and an executive summary
What the report tracks and why it matters, written for the request that was made.
Strategic insights
What to watch and which thresholds matter, to read the report with.
The formulas (DAX measures)
Totals, averages, counts and ratios, installed in the Power BI dataset so every chart and every later report can use them.
The page layout
KPI cards on top, charts in a grid below, titled and sorted. Extra pages are added when there are more charts.
The charts, eleven kinds to choose from
The AI picks the chart that fits each question.
  • KPI card
  • Column
  • Bar
  • Line
  • Area
  • Donut
  • Pie
  • Funnel
  • Gauge
  • Table
  • Matrix
A preview in Odoo, before publishing
The key figures are calculated from the Odoo records right away, so the numbers can be checked before the report goes to Power BI.

Safe by design

The AI drafts. Your team decides, and the module checks.

The AI sees the shape of the data, never the data.

To draft a report, Gemini receives table and column names. Customer names, invoices and amounts are not sent.

Sent to the AI
  • Table names
  • Column names and types
  • Links between tables
  • Existing formulas
Never sent
  • Customers and suppliers
  • Invoices and orders
  • Amounts and quantities
  • Any other record

You approve first

Nothing reaches Power BI until someone clicks Build & Push.

Only real fields

Every formula is checked against the actual tables and columns. Invented names are caught before Power BI sees them.

No broken charts

A chart that can’t be matched to real data is left out and listed, never published empty.

No blank reports

Publishing stops if a table has never received data, and says what to do.

Hand-built reports are safe

A report someone made in Power BI is never overwritten.

Every step recorded

Each publish is logged, and any failure is explained in plain words.

What it needs

A one-time setup, then it is ready for every report.

  • A Google Gemini API key, from Google AI Studio (a free tier is available), entered once in the Power BI settings.
  • A Power BI Pro licence for the account that publishes.
  • One Microsoft permission to create reports, approved once by an administrator.
  • Data sent to the dataset, so the new report has real figures to show.

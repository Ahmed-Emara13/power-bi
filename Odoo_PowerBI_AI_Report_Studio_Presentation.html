How it works

From a request to a live report in five steps.

  1. Describe it

    Pick the dataset and the design, then write what you want in plain language.

  2. The AI reads the dataset

    Google Gemini studies its tables, columns and links, and drafts a report for the request.

  3. Review the blueprint

    See every formula and chart before anything is published. Untick what you don’t want.

  4. Build & Push

    One click installs the formulas and creates the report in Power BI, bound to your data.

  5. Open and reuse

    Open it in Power BI or inside Odoo, and save the design for the next client.

What the AI prepares

A complete report, not a list of suggestions.

Title and summary
What the report tracks and why it matters, written for the request.
Strategic insights
What to watch and which thresholds matter when reading it.
The formulas (DAX)
Totals, averages, counts and ratios, installed in the Power BI dataset for reuse.
The page layout
KPI cards on top, titled and sorted charts below, extra pages when needed.
A preview in Odoo

New · Outside designs

Found a design you like? Drop the file in, Odoo does the rest.

  1. Download it

    A theme from a gallery or generator, a Power BI report or template, or one saved from Power BI Desktop.

  2. Upload Design Files

    Drop several files at once:

    • .json
    • .pbix
    • .pbit
    • .zip

  3. Odoo reads it

    It takes the theme out of reports and packs, and turns each one into a design.

  4. Ready for every report

    Preview it, put it on any background, make it the default in one click.

  • ✓Copy-paste marks, comments and stray commas removed
  • ✓Misplaced styles moved to where Power BI reads them
  • ✓Duplicates skipped, only the report’s own theme kept
  • ✓A wrong file is named, with what to do about it

Safe by design

The AI sees the shape of the data, never the data.

To draft a report, Gemini receives table and column names. Customer names, invoices and amounts are not sent.

Sent to the AI
  • Table names
  • Column names and types
  • Links between tables
  • Existing formulas
Never sent
  • Customers and suppliers
  • Invoices and orders
  • Amounts and quantities
  • Any other record

Safe by design

The AI drafts. Your team decides, and the module checks.

You approve first

Nothing reaches Power BI until someone clicks Build & Push.

Only real fields

Every formula is checked against the actual tables and columns before Power BI sees it.

No broken charts

A chart that can’t be matched to real data is left out and listed, never published empty.

No blank reports

A table Power BI holds no data for is sent first, automatically, before the report.

Hand-built reports are safe

A report someone made in Power BI is never overwritten.

Every step recorded

Each publish is logged, and any failure is explained in plain words.

What it needs

A one-time setup, then it is ready for every report.

  • A Google Gemini API keyFrom Google AI Studio (a free tier is available), entered once in the Power BI settings.
  • A Power BI Pro licenceFor the account that publishes the reports.
  • One Microsoft permissionTo create reports, approved once by an administrator.
  • Data sent to the datasetSo every new report has real figures to show.
